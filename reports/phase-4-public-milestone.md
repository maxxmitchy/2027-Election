# Phase 4 Public Milestone

## Public post

We're now measuring not just what the database knows, but where its evidence is strong, where it's thin, which claims remain disputed, and where the primary record is missing. The next layer is knowing why we don't know something.

Create a sophisticated editorial illustration for a serious nonfiction technology/evidence project titled “EVIDENCE COVERAGE”. Show three completed candidate dossier frames—Bola Ahmed Tinubu, Peter Gregory Obi and Atiku Abubakar—without political campaign styling or glorification. Each dossier should be represented as a transparent research case file containing layered evidence: official election records, economic datasets and charts, court documents, government records, public statements, X/social-media artifacts, source provenance, correction trails, contradictory evidence and review markers.

Around the three dossiers, build an evidence-coverage map rather than a ranking. Some areas should be visibly dense and well documented; others should be thinner, marked PARTIAL or SPARSE; some should show DISPUTED or UNKNOWN; and some should be visibly empty research gaps labeled PRIMARY SOURCE UNAVAILABLE or EVIDENCE GAP. Use neutral labels such as WELL DOCUMENTED, PARTIAL, SPARSE, DISPUTED, UNKNOWN and UNAVAILABLE. Do not assign percentages that imply truth probability. Do not use green/red political scorecards.

At the center, place a precise research-control interface labeled:

“WE ARE MEASURING THE EVIDENCE, NOT JUST COLLECTING IT.”

Visually connect each coverage area to its underlying source chain: CLAIM → EVIDENCE → SOURCE, and for quantitative material: RESULT → CALCULATION → OBSERVATION → DATASET VERSION → SOURCE VERSION. Include small provenance tags, version indicators, correction arrows and contradiction links. Show a subtle secondary layer representing research gaps and missing primary records rather than hiding them.

A restrained warm-white or very light neutral background, generous whitespace, sophisticated editorial typography, precise grid alignment, documentary textures, subtle data visualization, restrained professional color palette, premium business-book aesthetic, intellectually serious, realistic but conceptual. No candidate portraits, flags, campaign logos, slogans, propaganda, polling numbers, invented statistics or political endorsements. The image should communicate one idea clearly: the system is becoming capable of measuring the state of its documentary knowledge, including its limits and gaps.
