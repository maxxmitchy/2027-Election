# Phase 5 — From Question to Auditable Answer

The 2027 Election evidence system has entered its research-workbench phase.

Instead of treating a difficult research question as a single search, the workbench breaks it into explicit sub-questions, identifies the evidence required to establish each claim, targets primary sources, records documentary gaps, preserves contradictions, and creates deterministic research tasks.

The system does not turn incomplete evidence into certainty. It distinguishes what is documented, what remains unverified, what is disputed, what is unavailable, and what requires human review.

The workbench is deliberately limited to the three existing validated research subjects: Bola Ahmed Tinubu, Peter Gregory Obi, and Atiku Abubakar. Candidate 4 remains blocked.

The principle is simple:

> What would we need to know to answer this honestly?

Phase 5 is a research-process milestone, not a political ranking, scorecard or recommendation system.
