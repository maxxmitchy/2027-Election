# Evidence Display

The default view is sparse. It prioritizes the direct answer and evidence status.

Evidence expands on demand and may expose source, source tier/date, relationship, claim, archive status, version and hash where present.

### Quantitative answers

Display exact inputs with value, unit, period and source, followed by formula and result. Percentage-point changes are explicitly labelled as such.

### Causal answers

Separate temporal relationship from direct causal evidence, alternative explanations and individual attribution. The default conservative result is `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` when personal causation is not established.

### Contradictions

Show the competing positions and their sources. Do not manufacture a winner.

### Corrections

Show previous record → correction → current record. Historical state remains inspectable.

### Public conversation

Statements are visually and semantically distinct from independent evidence. A statement establishes occurrence of the statement, not truth of its content.

### Negative knowledge

Controlled statuses such as `UNKNOWN`, `UNVERIFIED`, `DISPUTED`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, `INCOMPLETE` and `INCOMPARABLE` are retained and explained.
