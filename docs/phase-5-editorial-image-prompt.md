# Editorial Image Prompt — Phase 5

Create a sophisticated editorial technology/evidence illustration titled **“FROM QUESTION TO AUDITABLE ANSWER.”**

Show one complex research question entering a transparent evidence-investigation system. The visual chain should be clearly legible as:

**QUESTION → CLAIMS → EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS → SOURCES → EVIDENCE GRAPH → CONTRADICTIONS / GAPS → REVIEW → AUDITABLE ANSWER**

The system should feel like a high-integrity research laboratory: structured data panels, provenance trails, source documents, version markers, evidence links, contradiction indicators, research-gap cards and a human review checkpoint. Emphasize traceability, uncertainty and disciplined investigation rather than speed or certainty.

Use a sophisticated editorial technology aesthetic: restrained palette, premium information-design composition, subtle depth, clean typography, archival/documentary textures and precise graph-like connections. The central visual metaphor is a question being progressively constrained and evidenced until it becomes an auditable answer.

Do **not** depict candidate portraits, campaign imagery, flags, polling, rankings, endorsements, voting recommendations or truth percentages. Do not imply political preference. The subject is the **research process**, not the candidates.
