# Phase 8 — Dossier Review, Quality Assurance & Publication Control

**IMPLEMENTED:** Deterministic review records, typed reviewer capability, claim-level review state, provenance/temporal/quantitative audits, publication-readiness controls, immutable publication metadata, publication diff/recall controls and Phase 8 mutation testing.

**EXECUTED:** The controlled Phase 8 CI workflow runs the Phase 3–7 regressions, consumes the Phase 7 dossier set, executes the controlled review set, evaluates publication readiness independently for the three validated candidates, exercises publication recall/diff controls and runs 40 mutations.

**QUALIFIED / PUBLISHED:** These are distinct states. A dossier is not called verified or published merely because it was assembled. Publication occurs only after the defined publication policy is satisfied.

**PASS:** Phase 8 must not be described as PASS until the exact final CI execution passes all fail-closed gates and produces its evidence artifact.

The governing boundary remains: evidence establishes; research investigates; review evaluates the evidence record; publication certifies that a dossier satisfies its defined quality requirements. None of these means political truth, merit or electability.
