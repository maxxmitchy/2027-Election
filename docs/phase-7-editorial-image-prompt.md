# FROM EVIDENCE TO LIVING DOSSIER

Create a sophisticated editorial-quality conceptual illustration titled **FROM EVIDENCE TO LIVING DOSSIER**.

Show a deterministic research pipeline transforming a set of structured research tasks into a versioned candidate dossier. Visually separate: research tasks → acquired evidence → source verification → claim graph → review queue → versioned dossier. Make corrections, contradictions, unresolved research gaps, unavailable evidence, provenance links, and version history visibly distinct layers.

Use a serious nonfiction research aesthetic: warm neutral background, restrained typography, precise grid, generous whitespace, archival/documentary visual language, subtle technical interface elements, no campaign imagery.

Do not show candidate portraits, polling, rankings, scores, voting advice, party propaganda, slogans, flags as political branding, or an LLM/AI brain. The concept is an evidence-product architecture, not a political advertisement.

The dossier should visibly have V1 and V2 states, with an evidence update flowing into V2 while V1 remains preserved. Open gaps and review items must remain visible rather than being visually erased.
