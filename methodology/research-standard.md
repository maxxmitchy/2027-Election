# Research Standard

## Purpose

This repository is an evidence infrastructure for researching Nigeria's 2027 presidential election and related historical, political, governance and socioeconomic facts. It is not a campaign, advocacy or opinion project.

## Research sequence

1. Define the question or claim precisely.
2. Identify the relevant subject, date/period and geographic scope.
3. Search for primary evidence first.
4. Inspect the actual source; never cite an uninspected source.
5. Capture provenance, publication date and observation date separately.
6. Record the claim and evidence as structured data.
7. Seek corroboration for material claims.
8. Search for credible contradictory evidence.
9. Assess verification status and confidence.
10. Record calculations in reproducible form.
11. Preserve revisions and corrections rather than silently overwriting history.

## Evidence categories

Every substantive record should distinguish, where applicable: fact, allegation, statement, analysis, estimate, opinion, or disputed.

## Research discipline

Do not infer causation from temporal sequence alone. Do not fill missing fields with guesses. Preserve original units, definitions and wording where material. When a source is revised, superseded or corrected, retain the prior state and record the reason for change.

## Candidate research

Candidate dossiers should be populated only after the schemas and evidence workflow are established. Each biographical, political, legal, financial or policy assertion should be traceable to one or more evidence records.
