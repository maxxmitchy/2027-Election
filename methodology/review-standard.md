# Public Review Standard

Reviews are a separate layer from evidence. Community agreement never determines evidentiary truth.

## Review categories

- `accurate`
- `partially_accurate`
- `incorrect`
- `evidence_insufficient`
- `source_problem`
- `calculation_problem`
- `context_problem`

## Review requirements

A useful review identifies the affected record or claim, explains the issue, provides supporting evidence where available, and records its creation time. Reviews must not silently mutate evidence records.

## Adjudication

A review may trigger a new record version after verification. The original review remains preserved. Reviewer identity should be represented according to project privacy and governance policy; do not publish unnecessary personal information.

## Aggregation

Review counts and ratings may be displayed as signals about community assessment, but must never be represented as source quality, truth probability or corroboration.
