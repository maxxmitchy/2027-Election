# Bola Ahmed Tinubu — Gate 4 Pilot Dossier

**Pilot status:** controlled research fixture, not a complete public biography.

## Identity

Bola Ahmed Tinubu is the current President of Nigeria and the APC's nominated presidential candidate for the 2027 election as of the research cutoff of 30 August 2026. The State House identifies him as President and records his presidency as beginning on 29 May 2023, and records his eight-year Lagos governorship from 1999 to 2007.

Sources: https://statehouse.gov.ng/team/bola-ahmed-tinubu/ ; https://statehouse.gov.ng/acceptance-speech-by-president-bola-ahmed-tinubu-gcfr-on-his-nomination-as-apc-presidential-flagbearer-for-the-2027-election-delivered-on-sunday-may-24-2026/

The APC biography states that Tinubu attended Richard J. Daley College and Chicago State University and earned a bachelor's degree in 1979. Chicago State University independently confirmed his attendance and 1979 graduation in its 2023 records statement.

Sources: https://www.apc.com.ng/biography.php ; https://www.csu.edu/records_request.htm

## Political chronology

- 1992: elected to the Senate for Lagos West on the Social Democratic Party platform, according to the APC biography and historical reporting.
- 1999: elected Governor of Lagos State on the Alliance for Democracy platform.
- 2003: re-elected Governor of Lagos State; the result used in this pilot is a secondary-source result and is not treated as an authoritative electoral anchor.
- 2007: completed his second Lagos governorship term.
- 2013: associated with the formation of the All Progressives Congress through the merger of ACN, CPC, ANPP and an APGA faction.
- 2023: APC presidential candidate; INEC declared him elected with 8,794,726 votes.
- 2026: APC nominated Tinubu as its presidential flagbearer for the 2027 election.

Sources: https://www.apc.com.ng/biography.php ; https://guardian.ng/politics/the-tinubu-timelines-in-nigerias-politics/ ; https://www.apc.com.ng/about.php ; https://inecnigeria.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/2023-GENERAL-ELECTION-REPORT-1.pdf

## Economic pilot

Two directly reproducible inflation comparisons are included in the machine-readable records:

- December 2022 → December 2023 headline inflation: 21.34% → 28.92%, a **7.58 percentage-point increase**.
- December 2023 → January 2024 headline inflation: 28.92% → 29.90%, a **0.98 percentage-point increase**.

Sources: https://www.nigerianstat.gov.ng/elibrary/read/1241439 ; https://www.nigerianstat.gov.ng/elibrary/read/1241453

The pilot deliberately does not describe these changes as being caused by Tinubu merely because they occurred during his presidency.

For GDP, NBS reports 2023 annual real GDP growth of 2.74%; a later NISER report citing NBS reports 2024 growth of 3.40%. The pilot treats the latter as a separately sourced observation requiring dataset/version provenance rather than as proof of policy causation.

Sources: https://www.nigerianstat.gov.ng/pdfuploads/Q3_2024_GDP_Report.pdf ; https://niser.gov.ng/v2/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2024-Q3-Q4-N-SEW_final.pdf

For public debt, the DMO reports total public debt of ₦97.341 trillion at 31 December 2023. The pilot preserves the observation and does not infer that the president caused the change in debt.

Source: https://www.dmo.gov.ng/news-and-events/circulars-releases/4949-press-release-nigeria-s-total-public-debt-as-at-december-31-2023/file

For FX, the CBN describes its 14 June 2023 willing-buyer/willing-seller reform and subsequent NFEM framework. The pilot records the policy event separately from exchange-rate observations so temporal sequence is not converted into causal attribution.

Sources: https://www.cbn.gov.ng/intops/FXStructureAndManagement.html ; https://www.cbn.gov.ng/rates/ExchRateByCurrency.html

## Contradictory / qualifying evidence

The administration describes its reforms as improving macroeconomic stability and resilience. The IMF independently records improved macroeconomic outcomes while also stating that conditions remain difficult for many Nigerians, including substantial poverty and food-insecurity concerns. Reuters likewise reports the administration's stabilization claim alongside the cost-of-living squeeze. These records are represented as **qualifying/contextualizing evidence**, not as a forced binary choice.

Sources: https://statehouse.gov.ng/statement-by-president-bola-ahmed-tinubu-gcfr-on-the-third-anniversary-of-his-administration-may-29-2026/ ; https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2026/06/09/pr26190-nigeria-imf-executive-board-concludes-2026-article-iv-consultation-with-nigeria ; https://www.reuters.com/world/africa/nigerias-tinubu-says-reforms-stabilising-economy-despite-hardship-2026-05-29/
